🟦 1. Influencer Metadata (Required)

This is the backbone of the chatbot.

Feed the model:

Influencer name

Bio

Username

Niche/category

Followers

Following

Engagement rate

Average likes

Average comments

Reel average views

Contact info (optional)

Country & city

Gender (if available)

Audience demographics (if available)

➡ This enables the bot to answer:
“Give me a summary of this influencer.”

🟧 2. Post-Level Data (Important for analytics)

For each post:

Caption text

Likes count

Comments count

Views (reels)

Hashtags

Timestamp

Sponsored or not

This lets the chatbot:

detect most used hashtags

understand influencer personality/content

calculate engagement

recommend similar influencers

show trending content

Should you feed the whole captions?

✔ Yes — captions improve semantic understanding of niche, tone, and topics.

🟩 3. Hashtag Data

Include:

Hashtag → frequency for influencer

Hashtag → engagement impact

Hashtag clusters (niches)

This lets the chatbot answer:

“What are the trending hashtags for influencer X?”

“Find influencers in skincare niche.”

🟨 4. Brand Metadata

If your platform links influencers to brands:

Brand name

Category

Preferred influencer niche

Past collaborations

Campaign budgets (if stored)

So the bot can answer:

“Recommend influencers for Nike.”

“Show rising stars suitable for makeup brands.”

🟪 5. Computed Metrics (Essential for intelligence)

You MUST store and feed precomputed metrics:

Engagement rate

Growth rate

Monthly views

Posting frequency

Rising star score

Content categories (if you detect them)

These let the bot do reasoning without re-calculating.
